# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that supports a constructive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting specific, actionable feedback;
- focusing criticism on ideas and changes rather than people;
- acknowledging mistakes and taking responsibility;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality, and the project's synthetic-data boundary.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual
attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private
information, or submitting real customer, employer, credential, or personal data.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone represents the project in public,
including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project-related events.

## Enforcement

Report abusive or unsafe behavior to the maintainer through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/osmankaankars). Do not include sensitive personal
information in a public issue. You may also use GitHub's platform reporting tools for content that
violates GitHub policy.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions
that violate this code. Responses will be proportionate to the behavior and may include a warning,
temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces. Reports will be handled as
privately and fairly as practical.
